```text
https://chkhaidze.ucsd.edu/simLex/datasets/SimLex-999.txt
```

Primary paper:

```text
Hill, Reichart, and Korhonen (2015),
SimLex-999: Evaluating Semantic Models with (Genuine) Similarity Estimation.
Computational Linguistics 41(4), 665-695.
```

The benchmark is used only for post-hoc evaluation. Its scores are never used
to build or tune TreeHeap landing points.
